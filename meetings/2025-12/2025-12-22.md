# Business Meeting Summary

**Date:** December 22, 2025

---

## Pitch Deck Foundations

The team discussed how to assemble an investor pitch deck for Roko. Several interested parties have asked for a dry, straightforward business proposal stating how much is being raised and how funds will be used. A participant with associate-side pitch-deck experience offered to help structure a standard deck covering market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM), use of funds, valuation, competitors, and the problem and solution statements. The group acknowledged that Roko's hybrid PTP-timing-plus-blockchain model is novel territory and that there are no direct comparables — comps exist for PTP timing and for blockchain separately, but not for the combined model, which was framed as a blockchain primitive without established competition.

## Use of Funds & Development Tiers

The group agreed the deck must clearly show how investment would be deployed. The approach discussed is to start from tiered development estimates — what a roughly one-million versus a five-million spend would each deliver — and then apply those engineering figures to the existing business model so that peripheral costs (certification, auditing, advertising, business development, mainnet launch) are accounted for on top of raw development cost. The team noted that development is only a fraction of total spend, with the remainder going to these surrounding activities. A one-pager on use of funds, mapped against the DAO distribution model previously developed with the team's fund-management partner, was identified as the immediate priority.

## Collaboration Plan

The team agreed to use the holiday period to draft a template and circulate it. One contributor will work directly with the development lead to capture the development vision and associate timelines with each tier, targeting an initial draft around January 1st. In parallel, the contributor will spend time with the team's brand/creative lead to learn the storytelling approach behind Roko's public messaging, with the goal of carrying that voice into the deck. The team also discussed getting the contributor access to Roko's site, documents, and media assets, and looping in the external fund-management partner — who is being onboarded to an investment board and may help position Roko once a more mature project ahead of it is funded, with answers expected around the first quarter.

## Investment Philosophy

The group articulated a guiding principle for taking on investors: maintain management control of the project and the DAO, distribute influence across multiple investors rather than concentrating it, and avoid granting any single party veto power. The aim is to bring in investors who represent their interests while leaving development judgment with the core team closest to the work. The team also touched on its legal structure — a foundation that protects DAO assets and a separate corporation that handles contracts and developer distributions and is wholly owned by the foundation — noting that clarifying investor entry points into this structure is part of the work ahead.
